# Tips for players

Most of what makes a big tree manageable is already on the screen, it is just easy to miss. **Pinning** a few nodes you plan to do next and using the search to jump between them saves more time than any amount of scrolling, and pins are remembered between sessions. Before you go out and gather materials for a locked node, **hover it**. The lock message often names the real gate, which is sometimes a different node from the one you assumed.

The padlock on an item in your inventory, or in JEI and EMI, tells you which node unlocks it, so a pack's restrictions double as a map of what to research first. If you cannot work out where an item fits, press `Y` over it and the nodes that ask for or reward it are highlighted, and `Tab` walks through them.

For trees that need bulk items, building a small **automated feeder** next to the machine, a hopper line from a chest, is easier than carrying stacks back and forth. When several people play, **a team** lets one player work on the mining line while another does the farming line, with everything shared, and the **recent activity** list in the Codex shows what a teammate just unlocked.

Press **H** or **F1** inside the machine screen for the in-game help, which holds the same tutorials as this wiki. And if a large tree runs slowly on your PC, lowering the **screen FPS limit** and turning off the **ambient effects** in the [preferences](player/preferences) gives the biggest gain.
