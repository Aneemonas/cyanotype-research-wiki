# The Codex

The Codex is the overview of every tree you are allowed to see, and in Quest Book mode it is also how you open them.

::video player-codex "The Codex: cards, tabs, rank and recent activity"

At the top there is your **rank** with a progress bar and its milestones, and under it a row of tabs: All, In progress, Completed and Undiscovered, with sorting. Each tree gets a **card** with its own progress bar. Trees you have not opened yet look encrypted, and opening one for the first time marks it as discovered. A **recent activity** list records what happened during this session, such as nodes completed, nodes that became available, finished trees and messages from the machine, so it doubles as a catch-up after a teammate has been playing.

Finishing a whole tree plays a banner and a burst of particles, and finishing the **entire Codex** sets off fireworks.

## Opening a tree from the Codex

In *Quest Book* mode a click on a card opens that tree directly. In *Legacy* mode the tree is opened by the blueprint in the machine's central slot, and the Codex is only an overview, so a click on a card does not open anything there.

## Resource strip

Under the Codex banner, and also beside the lab level badge, a small strip shows the machine's gauges and a few chips: energy, fluid, Sources and the virtual currencies of the tree. Each group can be hidden in the client preferences if you find the strip crowded.
