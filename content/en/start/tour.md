# Five-minute tour

This is the path a new player takes, in order. Each step links to the page that covers it properly.

:::steps
1. **Place the machine.** Put a Research Station anywhere and right click it. It hums while you are near. See [the machine](player/machine).

   ::video tour-place-machine "Placing the Research Station and opening it"

2. **Open a tree.** In *Legacy* mode you put the tree's blueprint in the central slot and the tree unfolds. In *Quest Book* mode you pick the tree in the Codex instead. See [opening a tree](player/opening-trees).

   ::video tour-open-tree "A blueprint goes in and the tree unfolds level by level"

3. **Look around.** Drag to pan, scroll with the wheel, zoom with Ctrl + Alt + wheel and press `F` to fit the whole tree in view. See [moving around](player/navigation).

   ::video tour-navigation "Panning, zooming and searching a big tree"

4. **Read a node.** A locked node says why it is locked, and hovering or clicking any node lists its requirements and rewards. See [node states](player/node-states).

5. **Research it.** Hold the button until the ring fills. The items and XP are paid and the rewards arrive. See [researching](player/researching).

   ::video tour-research "Holding the button to complete a node, with the reward toast"

6. **Feed the machine.** Some nodes want items, fluids or energy delivered to the machine instead of carried by you, and a hopper, pipe or cable does the delivery. See [feeding](player/feeding).

   ::video tour-feeding "A hopper, a pipe and a cable feeding the machine"

7. **Check the Codex.** It shows the progress of every tree, with tabs, a rank and a list of recent activity. See [the Codex](player/codex).

8. **Play together.** Create a team and your progress is shared. See [teams](player/teams).
:::

## Building trees

All of it is data. Turn on dev mode with `/cyanotype dev true` and the same screen gains an editor, so the tree you have just played can be changed from inside. The next step is [your first tree in 10 minutes](dev/quick-start).
